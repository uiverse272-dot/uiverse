Chicken with preserved lemon and saffron

A tagine in spirit if not in pot. Thighs brown hard in one wide pan, then simmer in a broth the colour of late afternoon.

By Layla Haddad Saving a recipe needs Functional cookies, because that’s how we remember it.

You’ll need

  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 large pinch of saffron
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind only
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 120 g cracked green olives
  • A handful of parsley and sage

Method

  1. Bloom the saffron. Crumble it into 500 ml of hot stock and leave it while you do everything else. It should turn the colour of a marigold.
  2. Brown the chicken. Skin side down in a wide pan over a medium heat, undisturbed, for ten minutes. Lift out and pour off all but two spoons of fat.
  3. Soften the onions in the same pan with a pinch of salt until they slump, about twelve minutes, scraping up anything stuck.
  4. Simmer. Return the chicken, add the stock and lemon, cover and cook for twenty minutes. Stir in the olives and herbs for the last five.
